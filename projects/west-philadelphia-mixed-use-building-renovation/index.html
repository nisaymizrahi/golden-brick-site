West Philadelphia Mixed-Use Building Renovation

A completed building renovation with one commercial space and two three-bedroom residential units.

The Project

A Commercial Space and Two Renovated Apartments

We renovated a ground-floor commercial space and two apartments above it. Each apartment has three bedrooms and one bathroom.

The work included framing, electrical, plumbing, heating and cooling, drywall, flooring, lighting, and trim. Both apartments received updated kitchens, bathrooms, and living areas.
