What We Build

Custom Homes, Multifamily & Mixed Use

We review the site, proposed use, drawings, and finish requirements for each building.

  • New homes: residential construction from foundations to finishes, based on approved plans.
  • Custom homes: homes built around the owner’s plans, selected materials, and design details.
  • Multifamily buildings: buildings with multiple homes, shared utilities, common areas, and required inspections.

Ground-Up Project Types

Construction Planned Around the Building’s Use

A custom house, multifamily building, and mixed-use property each need a different plan for approvals, systems, access, finishes, and owner decisions.

Custom Homes & New Houses

Homes built to the approved plans, with materials, fixtures, and finishes selected by the owner.

Multifamily Buildings

Multiple residential units built alongside shared utilities, common areas, and required fire and life-safety systems.

Mixed-Use Construction

Residential and commercial spaces in one building, with construction planned for the requirements of each use.

New Construction Process

The Main Steps in a New Build

Let us know whether you are evaluating a site, developing plans, or ready to price approved drawings.

01 · Project Review

Confirm the Property

Address, ownership or contract status, intended use, unit count, site conditions, and project goals.

02 · Design Team

Coordinate the Plans

Review the required drawings with your architect, engineers, and other design professionals before pricing and approvals.

03 · Preconstruction

Define Scope & Schedule

Prepare written pricing and review materials, allowances, permits, site access, and the construction schedule.

04 · Construction

Build & Inspect

Build the foundations, structure, exterior, utilities, and interiors, with inspections at the required stages.

05 · Closeout

Finish the Building

Final inspections, punch list, cleanup, project documentation, and owner walkthrough.

New Construction FAQ

Questions About Building From the Ground Up

Does Golden Brick build custom homes and new houses?

Yes. Golden Brick takes on ground-up residential projects, including new houses and custom homes, when the property, design status, budget, schedule, and scope are a fit.

Do you build multifamily and mixed-use buildings?

Yes. We review ground-up multifamily and mixed-use projects. Share the property, planned unit count, commercial use if any, design status, and budget to discuss the work.

When should I contact Golden Brick?

Contact us while you are evaluating the site or developing plans, or when drawings are ready for pricing. Send what you have and tell us which decisions are still open.

Do I need an architect or engineer?

Ground-up projects typically require professional plans, engineering, zoning or code review, permits, and inspections. Golden Brick coordinates construction with the owner’s design professionals and approved documents.
