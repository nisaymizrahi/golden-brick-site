Kitchen Scope

What Goes Into a Kitchen Remodel

Before work begins, we review the layout, appliance sizes, cabinet plan, and materials. Those decisions set the locations for plumbing and electrical work and help us plan deliveries and installation.

Layout & Scope Planning

We review cabinet space, appliance placement, walkways, and any walls you want to change. We also account for repairs to adjoining floors and walls.

Materials and Finishes

Choose cabinets, counters, tile, fixtures, and flooring with the layout and budget in mind. We help identify which selections need to be made before ordering or installation.

Scheduling the Work

We schedule demolition, framing, plumbing, electrical work, cabinets, countertops, and finishes in the order the installation requires.

Site Protection

We discuss access, floor protection, dust control, and where materials will be stored, especially when the rest of the home remains occupied.

Project Oversight

We coordinate the trades and deliveries and discuss decisions that affect the work or schedule.

Final Walkthrough

We walk through the kitchen with you and review the completed work and any remaining items.

Owner-Occupied Homes

Discuss how you will manage cooking, storage, and access while the kitchen is out of use.

Before You Move In

If you want the kitchen renovated before moving in, share your closing and move-in dates so we can discuss the schedule.

For an investment property, share the intended use and finish budget. A kitchen may also be part of bathroom work or a full-home renovation.

  • Kitchen plus adjacent-room upgrades
  • Layout changes and finishes in adjoining rooms
  • Work planned alongside the other rooms being renovated
Kitchen Remodeling FAQ

Questions Homeowners Ask Before a Kitchen Estimate

Do you only handle full kitchen gut jobs?

No. We review both full renovations and focused kitchen remodels. Send photos and describe what you want to replace or keep so we can assess the work.

Can you help with layout and finish decisions?

Yes. We can review the layout, cabinet plan, and finish selections with you. If the work needs architectural or structural drawings, we discuss that before construction.

Do you work in occupied homes?

Depending on the work, yes. We need to discuss kitchen downtime, utility interruptions, dust, and access before deciding whether staying in the home is practical.

Do you remodel kitchens outside Philadelphia?

Yes. We work in Philadelphia and nearby suburbs including Bucks County, Montgomery County, Delaware County, and the Main Line.

What should I send when I request an estimate?

Include the property address, whether the home is occupied, what you want to change, and your timing and finish preferences.
