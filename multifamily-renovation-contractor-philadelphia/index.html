Philadelphia renovation services

Multifamily Renovation Contractor in Philadelphia

We renovate apartments and common areas in Philadelphia multifamily buildings. Before pricing the work, we review the needs of each unit alongside shared plumbing, electrical systems, entries, and other building repairs.

What to send us
  • Number of units and which ones are occupied.
  • Photos of units, common areas, and utility spaces.
  • Known building issues and previous inspection reports.
  • Available access dates and the order in which you need units finished.
Start with the building

Include repairs that affect the whole building

A bathroom renovation may connect to a plumbing stack serving other units. Electrical service, roof repairs, entry stairs, and basement moisture can also affect the full building. Include those items before pricing each apartment.

A work list for each apartment

Record which kitchens, baths, floors, doors, and fixtures will be kept or replaced. Avoid assuming every unit needs the same work.

Shared areas and systems

Identify common halls, stairs, entries, utility spaces, roof or exterior work, and shared plumbing or electrical repairs.

Construction schedule

Discuss vacant units, tenant access, utility shutoffs, inspections, and material storage. Those details help us determine whether the work can be completed in stages.

Working with your manager

Agree on access before scheduling crews

Your property manager can share repair records and arrange access with tenants. Include them in planning when the renovation affects occupied units or shared spaces.

Questions before you start

Questions about multifamily renovations

Can the work be completed in phases?

Sometimes. The answer depends on shared systems, access, occupancy, and whether each phase can be finished safely. We review those conditions before agreeing to a sequence.

Do you review buildings before purchase?

Yes, subject to access and availability. Send the unit count, occupancy details, photos, and your inspection deadline so we can discuss a construction review.

Can you renovate apartments between tenants?

Send the number of apartments, the repairs needed, and the dates they will be vacant. We can review the work and discuss our availability.
