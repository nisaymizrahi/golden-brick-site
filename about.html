Our Work

One contractor to manage the job

We plan and manage the construction: defining the work, scheduling trades, arranging required inspections, and keeping you informed as the job progresses.

Our work includes a two-bedroom home renovation in the Italian Market, a Cheltenham property prepared for resale, and a West Philadelphia building with commercial space and two apartments. You can see the work and read what each project involved in our project gallery.

What Golden Brick Does

Before construction
Discuss the property, visit when needed, and put the proposed work, price, and terms in writing.
During construction
Coordinate trades and inspections, protect the work area, share updates, and document changes.
At completion
Review remaining items, clean up, and walk through the project with you.
Who You Work With

Your project team and the people on site

We coordinate the carpenters, plumbers, electricians, HVAC contractors, and other trades involved in your project.

Your Golden Brick contact answers questions about the schedule, materials, and work on site. When something needs to change, we explain how it affects the work and cost.

  • The written agreement sets out our responsibilities and the work included.
  • Qualified trade contractors carry out work that requires a specialist.
  • We coordinate with designers, engineers, and inspectors when the work calls for it.
What Clients Can Expect

Understand the job before work begins

Your estimate and agreement explain the work, the price, and how changes will be handled. Take time to review them and ask questions.

Estimate and Contract

Your estimate and contract set out the work, price, payment schedule, and any allowances or exclusions.

Plans and Permits

We identify required drawings, permits, and inspections as part of planning the work.

Construction Schedule

We plan the order of work, inspection stages, deliveries, and material decisions.

Site Management

We coordinate deliveries and trades, protect the work area, and manage access and cleanup.

Documented Changes

We document changes to the agreed work and review them with you before proceeding.

Final Walkthrough

Remaining items are reviewed near completion, followed by cleanup and a final walkthrough.
