Questions before you start

Questions about investor renovations

Can you review a property before I buy it?

Yes, subject to access and availability. Send the property details and your inspection deadline first. The review focuses on construction and visible conditions; it does not replace an inspection, appraisal, or engineering report.

Can you estimate work before the plans are finished?

Yes. We can review the proposed work and discuss an initial estimate. We may need drawings, material choices, a site visit, or specialist advice before confirming the price.

Do you work with out-of-area owners?

Tell us where you are based and who can provide access to the property. We will discuss how to keep you updated and who will approve materials and changes.

Where do you work?

Philadelphia and the surrounding Pennsylvania suburbs, including Bucks, Montgomery, and Delaware Counties. Share the address so we can confirm the project is within our service area.
