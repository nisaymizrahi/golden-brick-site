Before you buy

Separate repairs from resale upgrades

Start with the condition of the roof, structure, plumbing, electrical, and heating and cooling systems. Then decide which layout changes and finishes to include in the renovation budget.

Check the building

Review visible roof, moisture, structural, electrical, plumbing, and HVAC concerns. Identify what needs a specialist or further investigation.

Define the finishes

Specify cabinets, countertops, flooring, tile, fixtures, and appliances. If an item has an allowance, check whether that amount covers the product you plan to buy and its installation.

Price the whole job

Include demolition, disposal, site protection, repairs, trade work, finishes, and the agreed permit responsibilities. Track the purchase price, financing, and holding costs separately from the construction estimate.

Questions before you start

Questions about renovating a flip

Can you visit a property before I buy it?

Yes, subject to access and availability. Send the address and inspection deadline first. A contractor walkthrough reviews visible renovation needs; it does not replace a home inspection or engineering evaluation.

Will you work with my lender’s renovation scope?

We can review your lender’s scope against the proposed construction work. Share their format, required documents, and draw schedule before the estimate is finalized.

Do you guarantee resale value or profit?

No. We estimate and carry out construction work. Use your broker, appraiser, and lender to assess sale value, financing, and the overall investment.
