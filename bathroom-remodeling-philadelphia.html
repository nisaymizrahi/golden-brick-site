Why Homeowners Reach Out

What to Settle Before Work Begins

A bathroom has many parts in a small space. These are the decisions we review with you before construction.

Layout and Materials

Confirm which fixtures stay in place, which will move, and the tile, vanity, and hardware you want.

  • Fixture sizes, finish choices, and delivery times
  • Fixture locations before plumbing and electrical work
  • The work and materials included in the estimate

Construction and Access

Bathrooms depend on the right sequence across demolition, rough-ins, waterproofing, tile, glass, paint, and fixture installation.

  • Plumbing, electrical, and ventilation sequencing
  • Waterproofing before tile installation
  • Bathroom access while the work is underway

Final Walkthrough

The final walkthrough is a chance to review the completed work and agree on any remaining items.

  • Review of completed work
  • Tile, fixtures, trim, and other finish details
  • A list of any remaining work
Bathroom Remodeling FAQ

Questions Homeowners Ask Before a Bathroom Estimate

Do you handle full bathroom rebuilds or smaller remodels too?

Both. Send the existing bathroom photos and describe what you want to change. We can review a complete renovation or a more focused shower, tub, vanity, or tile project.

Can you help with tile, fixture, and finish decisions?

Yes. We review the tile, vanity, fixtures, lighting, and delivery dates with you before installation.

Do you work in occupied homes?

It depends on the work and access to another bathroom. Tell us if this is your only bathroom so we can discuss the expected disruption before work begins.

Do you remodel bathrooms outside Philadelphia?

Yes. We work in Philadelphia and nearby suburbs including Bucks County, Montgomery County, Delaware County, and the Main Line.

What should I send when I request an estimate?

Include the property address, whether the home is occupied, what you want to change, and your timing and finish preferences.
