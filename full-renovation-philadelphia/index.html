Our Renovation Services

Work That Can Be Included

The written scope identifies which rooms, repairs, and finishes are included in your renovation.

FH

Full-Home Renovations

Renovations across the home, with trades and installations managed under one construction plan.

PR

Partial Home Renovations

Renovations limited to selected rooms or one floor of the house.

KT

Kitchen Remodeling

Kitchen layouts, cabinets, countertops, lighting, and installation.

BA

Bathroom Remodeling

Tile, waterproofing, vanities, fixtures, and plumbing or ventilation changes.

BS

Basement Finishing

Basement layouts, utilities, insulation, flooring, and finish work.

IN

Interior Renovations

Room layout changes, wall and ceiling repairs, trim, doors, and finishes.

FL

Flooring Installation

Flooring installation as part of a remodel or as a standalone update.

PD

Painting & Drywall

Wall and ceiling repairs, drywall installation, preparation, and paint.

CC

Custom Carpentry

Trim, finish carpentry, and custom interior details installed with care.
