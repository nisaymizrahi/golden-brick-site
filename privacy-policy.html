Privacy Policy

How We Handle Project Inquiry Information

This page explains how Golden Brick Construction collects, uses, and stores the information you share when you contact us about a project.

Effective September 28, 2026

Information We Collect

When you contact Golden Brick Construction, we may collect the information you choose to provide, including your name, email address, phone number, project address, project type, desired timing, property status, optional budget range, referral source, communication consent, and any project details you submit through our forms, calls, emails, or other communications.

How We Use Your Information

  • To review your project and follow up on your estimate request.
  • To contact you by phone, text message, or email about your request, project scope, scheduling, and next steps.
  • To prepare estimates, confirm whether we can take on the work, and coordinate with our team and service providers.
  • To send marketing emails, promotional texts, or similar updates only when you separately opt in to those communications.
  • To improve our website and how we respond to customers.

Communication Consent

When you submit a project inquiry, you’re asking us to call you about the project. The form also lets you choose whether we may text or email you about your request.

Submitting a project inquiry does not sign you up for marketing messages. Any marketing communications require a separate opt-in and are not a condition of purchase.

How To Opt Out

You can ask us to stop marketing communications at any time by contacting us at info@goldenbrickc.com or calling (267) 715-5557. If we send a message with unsubscribe or opt-out instructions, you may also use those instructions.

How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with service providers that help us operate the website, process inquiries, store records, or communicate with customers, but only as reasonably necessary to support our business operations.

Data Retention And Protection

We keep inquiry records for as long as reasonably needed for business, operational, legal, or recordkeeping purposes. While no method of storage or transmission is guaranteed to be perfectly secure, we use reasonable steps to protect information submitted through the website and our internal systems.

Website Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand which pages people visit and whether they use our contact links and inquiry forms. Google may receive browser and device information and use cookies. Our form tracking records actions such as starting or submitting a request; the inquiry details themselves are sent to our project inquiry system.

We also keep campaign information, such as the source of a visit, in your browser’s session storage and attach it to an inquiry when available. You can control cookies through your browser settings. Learn more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the effective date on this page.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your communication preferences, contact Golden Brick Construction at info@goldenbrickc.com or (267) 715-5557.

At A Glance

  • We use your contact details to respond to your project inquiry.
  • Submitting an inquiry does not sign you up for marketing updates.
  • Contact us to change your communication preferences.
  • You can discuss a project without opting in to marketing.
