The Golden Brick Process

What Happens After You Reach Out

We start with a conversation about your property, your plans, and your timing. Here’s how the work moves forward.

01 · Inquiry

Send Project Details

Leave your name and phone number. Add a few details about the property if you have them.

02 · Project Call

Confirm the Basics

We discuss what you want to change, your budget, and when you hope to start.

03 · Walkthrough

Visit the Property

We visit the property when needed to assess the work and discuss your options.

04 · Written Estimate

Define the Work

You receive the proposed work and price in writing, including what is and isn’t included.

05 · Construction

Construction & Walkthrough

We manage the work, keep you updated, and walk through the finished project with you.
