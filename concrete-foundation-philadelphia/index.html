The details behind a concrete job

More than the day
of the pour.

Every property is different. Here’s what we discuss so the scope reflects the work your site actually needs.

  1. 01 / Before work

    Site & access

    Dimensions, removal, delivery access, nearby surfaces, and the route people use while work is underway.

  2. 02 / Below the surface

    Preparation & forms

    The base, elevations, drainage, forming, and reinforcement appropriate to the agreed project and any plans.

  3. 03 / The concrete work

    Placement & finishing

    The pour, finish, edges, and joints, coordinated around access, weather, and the work being built.

  4. 04 / After the pour

    Cleanup & care

    Cleanup included in the scope and guidance on protecting the surface and when it can be used.

Can you replace just a sidewalk or build a new path?

Yes. Concrete work can be a standalone job. We handle sidewalk replacement, walkways, side paths, and other concrete areas without requiring a larger renovation. Share the location and approximate size so we can review the work.

Do you have a team specifically for concrete?

Yes. Golden Brick has a dedicated team for concrete jobs, including removal and replacement, preparation, formwork, placement, and finishing. We’ll discuss the work your property needs and put the proposed scope in writing.

What other concrete work do you handle?

We handle driveways, parking pads, patios, slabs, garage and basement floors, steps, landings, curbs, footings, and foundations. If you don’t see your job listed, choose “Other concrete work” in the form and describe what you have in mind.

Should I repair the concrete or replace it?

That depends on the condition of the concrete and what is causing the problem. We review visible damage, movement, drainage, and the existing base before discussing the scope. A structural foundation concern may need an engineer’s assessment before a repair approach is defined.

What affects the price of a concrete job?

Size is one factor. Removal and disposal, site access, excavation, base preparation, reinforcement, concrete thickness, finish, and structural requirements can also affect the price. We review your project before providing a written estimate.

How soon can the work start, and when can I use the concrete?

Scheduling depends on the scope, crew availability, access, weather, and any required approvals. Walking and vehicle-use timing also depend on the concrete and site conditions. We discuss scheduling and care instructions for your specific job rather than promise one timeline for every pour.

Do you work on occupied homes and investment properties?

Yes. Tell us if people are living at the property, whether it is vacant, or if you are under contract. We can discuss access, phasing, and how the concrete work fits into your renovation or rental plans.

Where do you work?

Philadelphia, Bucks County, Montgomery County, Delaware County, and the Main Line. Include the full project address so we can confirm availability for your location.
