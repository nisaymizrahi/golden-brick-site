Before Construction

Agree on the Work Before Construction

A useful estimate starts with the existing condition and the work you want included. We discuss related repairs, materials, access, and any unknowns that need further investigation.

If you plan to live in the house during construction, we also need to discuss kitchen and bathroom access, utility interruptions, dust, and the rooms affected.

Home Remodeling FAQ

Questions Homeowners Ask Before Choosing a Contractor

What kinds of remodeling projects do you handle for homeowners?

Golden Brick handles kitchens, bathrooms, basements, additions, full-home renovations, and full-gut remodels, including related repairs and finishes.

Do you work on occupied houses and Philadelphia rowhomes?

Yes. We review rowhome and occupied-home projects. Whether you can remain in the home depends on the rooms involved, utility interruptions, access, and safety during construction.

Can you help if I am still deciding between a basement, addition, or bigger renovation?

Yes. Tell us what space you need, your priorities, and the property condition. We can discuss the construction involved in each option before you decide what to pursue.

Do you only work in Philadelphia city limits?

No. Golden Brick also serves nearby suburbs, including Bucks County, Montgomery County, Delaware County, and the Main Line.
