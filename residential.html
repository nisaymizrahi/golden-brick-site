Before Construction

Start With the Property and Your Priorities

Tell us what needs to change, what you want to keep, and how you plan to use the property. Photos, drawings, or an inspection report can help us understand the work before a site visit.

Let us know whether you will stay in the home during construction. For an investment property, share the planned rental or resale use, your budget, and any closing or tenant deadlines.

Project Questions

Before You Request an Estimate

Do you take on individual rooms as well as larger projects?

Yes. We review kitchen and bathroom remodels as well as additions, full-home renovations, and new construction. Describe the work so we can discuss whether it fits our services and schedule.

Can I stay in the home during construction?

It depends on the work. Kitchen and bathroom access, utility interruptions, dust, and safe access all need to be reviewed before deciding whether staying is practical.

Do you work with real estate investors?

Yes. We work with property owners and investors on renovations and construction. Tell us the planned use, property condition, timing, and the work you want priced.

Where do you work?

Philadelphia, the Main Line, Bucks County, Montgomery County, and Delaware County. We confirm availability based on the project address, scope, and schedule. View our service areas.

Tell Us About Your Project

Send the address, a brief description of the work, and your preferred timing. We will review the request and contact you to discuss the next step.
