Home Addition FAQ

Common Questions About Philadelphia Additions

Do home additions in Philadelphia usually need both zoning and building permits?

Philadelphia additions generally need zoning and building permits. The approvals depend on the property and proposed work. Review the City’s addition permit guidance and confirm the requirements for your address.

Can you combine an addition with a kitchen remodel or first-floor reconfiguration?

Yes. We can plan the addition and connected rooms as one project, including flooring, lighting, utilities, and the openings between spaces.

Are rowhome additions possible?

They may be possible, depending on the site, zoning, structure, and design. Shared walls, neighbor conditions, and access need to be considered with the design team.

How should I prepare before asking for an addition estimate?

Have the address, basic goals, photos, and any known constraints ready. It also helps to explain whether the home is occupied, whether the addition changes a kitchen or bedroom layout, and whether you already have plans started.
