Planning a rental rehab

Plan repairs before choosing finishes

Before choosing flooring or cabinets, look at leaks, aging equipment, ventilation, doors, stairs, and the condition of the electrical and plumbing systems. Decide which repairs are needed before the property is occupied.

Choose materials you can maintain

Choose flooring, hardware, cabinets, and fixtures that are easy to clean and replace. Keep the product names and model numbers for future repairs.

Address recurring problems

Share the maintenance history. Repeat leaks, moisture, and heating or cooling complaints deserve attention while the rooms are open.

Prepare for completion

Before completion, review any remaining work, cleanup, keys, and product information with your property manager.

Questions before you start

Questions about rental renovations

Can you renovate an occupied rental?

It depends on the work, access, utility interruptions, and whether occupied areas can remain safe and usable. We need those details before proposing a schedule.

Can you prepare a rental for refinancing?

We can estimate and complete the agreed renovation work. Share the lender’s property requirements and deadlines; loan approval and valuation remain with the lender.

Can I add bedrooms or change the number of units?

Discuss the proposed use before planning the renovation. Unit count and room use can raise zoning, building, and occupancy questions that need to be resolved for the specific property.
