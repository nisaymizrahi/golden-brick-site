Before Construction

Start With the Property and Your Plans

Here’s what to expect before work begins. The time needed for each step depends on the property, plans, permits, and material selections.

1. Tell Us About Your Project

Tell us where the property is, what you want to change, and when you hope to start. Photos, plans, and a budget range help us prepare for the first conversation.

2. Talk With Our Team

We contact you to talk through the work, your budget and timing, and whether a site visit is the next step.

3. Property Walkthrough

At the property, we review the visible conditions, take measurements, and discuss the rooms and systems involved. We also note what needs more investigation.

4. Review the Work and Budget

We discuss the work by room or trade, along with your priorities and budget. Together, we identify what to include in the estimate.

5. Written Estimate

Your written estimate describes the work and price, what is excluded, and any allowances for materials you haven’t selected yet. It also notes details that still need to be confirmed.

6. Contract and Payment Schedule

We review the scope, contract terms, payment schedule, and responsibilities with you before signing and starting work.

Planning and Construction

During Construction

7. Plans and Permits

We identify the plans, permits, and inspections the job needs and coordinate with the relevant professionals. We account for required approvals when planning the schedule.

8. Schedule and Material Selections

We plan the order of work and let you know when material selections are needed. Cabinets, fixtures, and other items that take time to arrive can affect the schedule.

9. Updates and Change Orders

We keep you informed about progress and decisions that need your attention. If you request a change or we find an unexpected condition, we put the proposed work, cost, and timing in writing for your review.

10. Final Walkthrough

We walk through the work with you, identify remaining items, and complete the final steps, including cleanup and any required inspections.
